Every year, 250,000 patients die from medical errors in the US alone — making it the third leading cause of death. Many of these are preventable: wrong drug prescribed to an allergic patient, dangerous drug combinations, missed lab values.

SCENE 2 What Is ClinicalGuard 0:20 – 0:50
Show the GitHub repo README or architecture slide
ClinicalGuard is a 27-tool clinical safety agent built on Google's Agent Development Kit, connected to the Prompt Opinion healthcare platform via the A2A protocol.

It doesn't guess. It doesn't hallucinate. Every single clinical fact comes from the patient's real FHIR health record — medications, allergies, lab values, vital signs — pulled live from the hospital's data.

All 27 tools are always available, but the agent intelligently decides which to run based on the patient's profile — their age, conditions, medications, and lab results. For an 82-year-old on 10 drugs, it runs Beers Criteria, fall risk, polypharmacy. For a healthy 35-year-old, it correctly skips those and reports exactly why. Things that would take a pharmacist hours, done in under 60 seconds.
SCENE 3 4-Layer Architecture 0:50 – 1:30
Show architecture diagram or bullet points on screen
What makes ClinicalGuard different from other AI safety tools? Four layers of anti-hallucination.

Layer 1 — Truth Tools. Ten tools that fetch verified data directly from the FHIR server. The agent physically cannot make up patient data.

Layer 2 — Intelligence Tools. Sixteen coded clinical protocols — not LLM opinions, but deterministic rule engines. The agent reads the patient's profile and decides which screens apply. Beers Criteria? Only if the patient is 65 or older. Heart failure therapy gaps? Only if they have heart failure. And for every screen it skips, it tells you why — "Beers Criteria: Not applicable, patient age 34 less than 65." When it DOES flag something, it cites the exact FDA guideline and the exact rule that triggered it.

Layer 3 — Negative Space Reporting. Most AI tools only report problems. ClinicalGuard explicitly reports what it checked and found clean. In medicine, proving you checked is as important as finding a problem. This eliminates alert fatigue.

Layer 4 — Multi-Model Verification. After the primary analysis, a second independent AI model reviews every finding. If the two models disagree, an arbitration loop resolves it — or flags it for human expert review. Two models agreeing independently is far more trustworthy than one.

SCENE 5 Why ClinicalGuard Wins 2:50 – 3:20
Show bullet points or return to face/slide
To summarize why ClinicalGuard is the best solution for this challenge:

One — Zero hallucination. Every clinical fact is FHIR-verified. Every finding cites a specific guideline.

Two — 27 tools, intelligently gated. The agent decides which screens apply based on the patient's age, conditions, and medications — and reports why it skipped the rest. No unnecessary alerts, no wasted checks.

Three — Multi-model verification. Two independent AI models must agree before a finding reaches a clinician.

Four — Negative space reporting. Clean screens are reported explicitly — reducing alert fatigue and building trust.

Five — Built on open standards. Google ADK, A2A protocol, FHIR R4, and deployed on the Prompt Opinion platform. This isn't a demo — it's production-ready architecture.

And one more thing — these aren't random screens we picked. Our 22 safety checks map to the 14 core Medication Therapy Management domains defined by ASHP and CMS — the same checks a hospital pharmacist performs during a clinical review. Drug-allergy conflicts, Beers Criteria from the American Geriatrics Society, renal dosing via CKD-EPI, sepsis screening via qSOFA, NEWS2 early warning — these are published, peer-reviewed clinical standards. Together, they cover over 90% of preventable adverse drug events according to published literature. Future versions will add hepatic dosing, anticholinergic burden scoring, and pregnancy safety — but what's here today is already hospital-grade coverage.

ClinicalGuard doesn't replace doctors. It gives them superhuman safety coverage in 60 seconds. Thank you.
